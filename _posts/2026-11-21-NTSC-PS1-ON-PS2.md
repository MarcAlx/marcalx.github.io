# Play NTSC-J / NTSC-U PS1 games on a 70004 PAL PS2 (Slim)

PlayStation 2 consoles are region-locked for PS1 game discs, PS2 game discs, and DVDs. This means that a PAL console cannot normally play official NTSC PS1 or PS2 games.

In this tutorial, I'll explain how to play official games from other regions without installing a modchip, specifically on a 70004 PAL PS2 (Slim).

## Introduction

This tutorial explains how to bypass the region check on a 70004 PAL PS2 (Slim) to play official NTSC PS1 game discs.

### The 70004 PAL PS2 case

MechaPwn, the homebrew utility commonly recommended to unlock your PS2 disc drive, cannot directly enable official PS1 games from other regions on this particular PS2 model.

From [MechaPwn's GitHub repository](https://github.com/MechaResearch/MechaPwn):

> How to play PS1 games from the different regions on PAL/NTSC-J FAT2 and 70k3 (original and backups)?
>
> You can do this only via the swap trick. For example, you can burn *ImportPlayer Light 2.1 for either PAL or Japan (according to your console's original region) and follow the instructions for disk swap in the program. Keep in mind that game compatibility will not apply and video mode should be set with *ImportPlayer Light 2.1.

_Note: The swap trick is a technique that involves launching a PS1 game recognized by the PS2. Once the PS2 has accepted the disc, you swap it for another game that the console would normally reject due to its region lock._

_The tricky part is that opening the PS2 disc cover normally interrupts the game. To bypass this behavior, you either need to physically modify the disc cover mechanism or use software that keeps the PS2 running while the cover is open, such as ImportPlayer Light._

### TL;DR

The software-based region bypass requires ImportPlayer Light. Since this software is not a Sony-licensed PS1 game, you first need to unlock your disc drive using MechaPwn.

1. Unlock your PS2 disc drive using MechaPwn.
2. Burn ImportPlayer Light 2.2 (EU) to a CD-R.
3. Launch ImportPlayer Light 2.2 (EU) from the disc.
4. Insert your PS1 game when prompted.
5. Enjoy!

### Prerequisites

Besides your PS2, you'll need:

- A CD-R disc.
- A FreeMcBoot memory card.
- A USB storage device.
- ImportPlayer Light 2.2 (EU), not version 2.1.

## Step 1. Unlock your PS2 using MechaPwn

To launch ImportPlayer Light, which is not a licensed PS1 game, you need to unlock your PS2 disc drive.

Without doing so, you'll either get the PS2 red screen or a black screen if you try to force-launch it through DKWDRV.

⚠️ First, carefully read the [MechaPwn GitHub repository](https://github.com/MechaResearch/MechaPwn). This operation is not risk-free.

1. Download the [MechaPwn development build](https://github.com/MechaResearch/MechaPwn/releases).
2. Copy it to a USB drive.
3. Boot your PS2 using FreeMcBoot.
4. Launch `uLaunch.elf`.
5. Navigate to `mass:` and run `MechaPwn_pck.elf`.
6. Back up your `NVRAM`.
7. Unplug your PS2 as instructed on screen.
8. Power your PS2 back on.
9. Launch `uLaunch.elf` again.
10. Navigate to `mass:` and run `MechaPwn_pck.elf` again.
11. Select `Retail-DEX`.
12. Select `Europe (Multi-7)`.
13. Keep the current patch.
14. Select **No** when asked about force unlock.

## Step 2. Burn ImportPlayer Light 2.2

ImportPlayer Light must be burned onto a CD-R disc. Here's how I did it.

1. Get ImportPlayer Light 2.2. For some reason, version 2.1 doesn't work for me. The image should be approximately 19.8 MB in size.
2. Burn it to a CD-R. For this tutorial, I'll use [cdrdao](https://cdrdao.sourceforge.net).
3. Identify your disc drive using:

```bash
cdrdao scanbus
```

4. Simulate the write operation:

```bash
cdrdao simulate \
  --device "<VALUE GOT FROM CDRDAO SCANBUS>" \
  --driver generic-mmc \
  --speed 4 \
  "ImportPlayer Light v2.2 (Europe).cue"
```

_Note: If your disc drive doesn't support 4x speed, `cdrdao` will automatically use a higher supported speed. 10x works fine for me._

5. If the simulation succeeds, proceed with the actual write operation:

```bash
cdrdao write \
  --device "<VALUE GOT FROM CDRDAO SCANBUS>" \
  --driver generic-mmc \
  --speed 4 \
  --eject \
  "ImportPlayer Light v2.2 (Europe).cue"
```

## Step 3. Play your game

1. Insert your burned ImportPlayer Light v2.2 disc into your PS2.
2. Start your PS2.
3. In ImportPlayer Light v2.2, select `Start game`.
4. When prompted to swap discs, insert your PS1 game.
5. Your game should now launch!

If you want to adjust video settings, you can also launch your game through [DKWDRV](https://github.com/DKWDRV/DKWDRV).
